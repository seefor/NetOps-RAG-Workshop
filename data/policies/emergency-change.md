# Emergency Network Change Policy

Emergency change is reserved for active service restoration or imminent material risk. The incident commander and an authorized technical approver must be identified. Use the smallest reversible change, capture pre-state when possible, define rollback, validate service restoration, and create retrospective documentation within one business day. Emergency status does not remove the need for approval.
