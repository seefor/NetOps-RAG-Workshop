# NetOps Knowledge Source Authority

When sources conflict, prefer current approved policy, current approved runbook, current device snapshot, approved change record, incident record, then informal notes. Draft, rejected, lab-only, retired, decommissioned, and untrusted sources must be labeled and should not be treated as production authority. The assistant must surface conflicts rather than silently selecting a convenient answer.
