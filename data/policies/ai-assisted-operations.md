# AI-Assisted Network Operations Policy

AI may summarize, retrieve, correlate, and propose read-only validation steps using approved knowledge. AI output is advisory. It may not approve changes, claim execution, expose secrets, or replace deterministic validation. Production modifications require an authenticated tool, authorization, change control, human review, and auditable execution.
