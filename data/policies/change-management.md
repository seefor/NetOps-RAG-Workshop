# Production Network Change Management

Every production network change must include scope, business reason, affected devices and services, risk, implementation steps, validation steps, rollback steps, maintenance window, owner, peer reviewer, and approval reference. The engineer must capture pre-change state and post-change validation. Emergency changes follow the emergency-change policy and still require retrospective documentation.
