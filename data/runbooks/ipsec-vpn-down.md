# Site-to-Site IPsec VPN Down

Validate internet reachability, peer address, IKE phase, proposal compatibility, identity, authentication status, IPsec transform settings, interesting traffic selectors, NAT exemption, and time synchronization. Never expose pre-shared keys in tickets or AI prompts. Collect sanitized negotiation logs before proposing changes.
