# VLAN or Trunk Mismatch

Confirm the expected access VLAN, voice VLAN, trunk mode, native VLAN, and allowed VLAN list on both ends. Check whether the VLAN exists locally and is active. Compare the distribution and access switch uplinks. Do not add a VLAN to every trunk as a shortcut; determine the intended path and scope first.
