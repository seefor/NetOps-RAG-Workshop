# DHCP Scope Exhaustion

Check scope utilization, lease duration, abandoned addresses, exclusions, reservations, relay configuration, and recent client growth. Verify the affected VLAN and helper address. Do not shorten lease duration or expand the scope without confirming address plan capacity and change approval.
